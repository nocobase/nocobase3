Add a departments example on top of the authorization example's sales and delivery domain: a department tree with memberships and a primary department, disabled rather than deleted, registered with authorization as the `org.department` inherited subject type. Settings → Departments, a notice-marked example page in the permission workspace's style, manages the tree, members and each department's region, fully localized in English and Chinese; seeded department titles are stored as translation descriptors. The seed adds an example trading company, six demo accounts sharing one password, the authorization example's permission sets, sharing and restriction rules assigned to departments and people, and each account's sales region. Membership and region changes keep the authorization example's sales-member regions in sync, so organisation attributes feed its "own region" data scope. It requires `@nocobase/app-plugin-authorization-example`, registered before it. Departments also have a head (`managerId`), edited in the Basic info tab with a user picker; every head of an active department holds the fixed `org.departmentHead` subject type, and a head change notifies the previous and new heads. Three department data scopes select the sales example's projects and quotes by their owner's department, and orders through their project's owner: `org.myDepartments`, `org.myDepartmentsAndBelow`, and `org.selectedDepartment` with `{ departmentId, includeDescendants }` params. The seed adds a department head permission set assigned once to the heads subject, a department project viewer set for North Sales and Delivery, a sharing rule that shares Sales Center's projects with Delivery, two head accounts, and places the authorization example's salespeople in the sales departments. It works with the authorization plugin alone: the seed skips sharing-rule and restriction-rule rows when those optional plugins are not installed.
