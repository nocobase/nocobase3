Add a departments example on top of the authorization example's sales and delivery domain: a department tree with memberships and a primary department, disabled rather than deleted, registered with authorization as the `org.department` inherited subject type. Settings → Departments, a notice-marked example page in the permission workspace's style, manages the tree, members and each department's region, fully localized in English and Chinese; seeded department titles are stored as translation descriptors. The seed adds an example trading company, six demo accounts sharing one password, the authorization example's permission sets, sharing and restriction rules assigned to departments and people, and each account's sales region. Membership and region changes keep the authorization example's sales-member regions in sync, so organisation attributes feed its "own region" data scope. It requires `@nocobase/app-plugin-authorization-example`, registered before it.
